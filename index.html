Asbuilt

Not everyone works in Gmail. In construction, many teams still run on older email systems with no AI built in, and important information gets lost in the inbox.

Asbuilt brings AI search to those inboxes. Ask a question in plain language and get an answer backed by the exact emails it came from.

  • 19,800emails in a real contractor inbox it was tested on
  • 4¢cost per question
  • ~500KU.S. specialty subcontractors it could help
  • 8+construction tech startups researched

UNIQLO

The Virtual Styler

open in Figma ↗

New shoppers struggle to picture full outfits from UNIQLO's minimal product photos, so they hesitate to buy or end up returning items.

The Virtual Styler is a drag-and-drop tool that previews an outfit head to toe. The deck covers users and pain points, mobile designs, success metrics like conversion and return rate, a go-to-market plan, and risks.

product case 02

Spotify

Profile customization

open in Figma ↗

Spotify's mission is to unlock human creativity, but listeners have almost no way to express their own. That gap limits engagement and gives people little reason to choose Spotify over other platforms.

I proposed profile customization as a near-term feature, letting users change profile colors, add picture decorations and show favorite songs, with a longer-term rhythm game studio. The slides walk through the full flow screen by screen.
